Biography

Kanye Omari "Ye" West, son of Donda and Ray West, born June 8th 1977, is a African American rapper most recognized for his talents in music and fashion design. Ever since he was a kid, he had a passion for production and rap music, taking inspiration from artists such as Jay-Z, MC Hammer, and De La Soul. He started his music career strong, releasing grammy-winning albums that raised the bar for what hiphop at the time (and today) should sound like. Since 2004, he's been releasing music consistently, and those project will be discussed in this site.
