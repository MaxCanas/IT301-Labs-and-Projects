The College Dropout (2004)

This is Kanye's debut album. His blend of chipmunk soul sampling, commentary on culture/politics, and unique style of rapping earned him 2 grammys. Widely regarded as one of the best debut albums of all time.

Late Registration (2005)

Following the success of Kanye's first album, Late Registration was dropped to serve as a sequel of his college themed discography. He used orchestral/string arrangements while expanding on the themes of family and culture previously rapped about in the first album. This album was very successful commercially, winning him another grammy for rap album of the year.

Graduation (2007)

This is the third and final album of Kanye's college bear trilogy. He raps about his accomplishments and the fame that followed him from the success of his other music. He uses more hype, stadium production with heavy drums in his songs. Graduation was strongly received, winning him 3 grammys (including rap album of the year again).

808s & Heartbreak (2008)

Following the death of his mother in 2007, and the breakup with his girlfriend at the time, Kanye hit a dark period emotionally and artistically. He expressed his emotions by touching on themes of abandonment and heartbreak with a completely different sound. His use of heavy 808s and singing inspired many current-day artists like Juice Wrld, The Weeknd, and Drake.

My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy (2010)

MBDTF is an ambitious, powerful album packed with strong performances from high-profile artists like Jay-Z, Rihanna, Rick Ross, Nicki Minaj, Pusha-T, Kid Cudi, and others. This albums maximalist, genre-blending production, paired with themes of triumph and success, earn it high regard from critics and fans. Most people call this album his greatest work. Ye's album was dropped after he spent time in self-isolation due to media backlash he was receiving for his behavior in the 2009 VMAs.

Yeezus (2013)

Yeezus takes the over-the-top sounds that MBDTF uses and explores how it can be used production-wise. He talks about religion, ego, and consumerism. The album's experimentation with industrial sound and 808s split Kanye's fanbase between people who wanted him to embrace this sound further, and those who missed the 'old Kanye.' Regardless, the album was well received.

The Life of Pablo (2016)

The actual life of Pablo means 3 different people to Kanye in this album: Saint Pablo, Pablo Escobar, and Pablo Picasso. Kanye sees himself in these individuals, even though they seem very polarized compared to one another. This polarization can be seen throughout the album where there are songs that are very religiously inspired, and others that are worldly inspired (sex, money, fame). The release of the album was very hectic with it only having been available and in Tidal originally, and the ablum getting over 30 revisions after release.

Ye (2018)

Ye is a highly personal project where Kanye talks about family, his recent mental health issues, and his conclusions on the self-reflection he'd been conducting up until that point. Most of the album was worked on and recorded in a barn in Wyoming. He uses more chill production throughout the EP.

Jesus is King (2019)

As the name suggests, this is a Christian-inspired album with no profanity or reference to profane topics (unlike his other projects). It wasn't as well received by his audience, who expected a completely different project at the time that had been delayed, and eventually scrapped. It did, however, win him a grammy for Christian album of the year.

Donda (2021)

This massive album contains 32 tracks (including the Deluxe music), all of which continue Kanye's theme of Christianity and zero-profanity. The rollout of this album was just as huge as the album itself, with Kanye hosting sold-out shows of the album that were days long. The list of featured artists in the album is also massive, with singers like The Weeknd, Tyler the Creator, Lil Baby, Baby Keem, Playboi Carti, Lil Durk, Travis Scott, Jay-Z, and so many more. The album touched on themes like redemption, faith, gospel, and family (especially after Kanye's divorce with Kim Kardashian). The album was very well received by critics and fans.

Donda 2 (2025)

This album features unfinished songs that didn't make it into Donda. The album also includes songs that were exclusively available on Kanye's Stem player that was being sold 3 years prior.

BULLY (2026)

BULLY is Kanye's last serious solo project (as of 2026) following the media backlash he was receiving for his streams of controversial remarks on Twitter. Kanye goes back to his classic style of rapping over soul sample beats as well as remaining fresh with 808s beats.
