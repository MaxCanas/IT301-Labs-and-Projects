Watch the Throne (2011)

Collaborative album between Jay-Z and Kanye. Very well received by critics and fans.

Kanye West Presents Good Music Cruel Summer (2012)

Collaborative album featuring artists signed to Kanye West's GOOD Music label.

KIDS SEE GHOSTS (2018)

Collaborative album between long time friend Kid Cudi and Kanye. They'd worked together on various projects prior.

VULTURES 1 (2024)

First collaborative album between Ty Dolla Sign and Kanye. Dropped in the midst of Kanye's controversies online.

VULTURES 2 (2025)

The second collaborative album between Ty Dolla Sign and Kanye. There was supposed to be a third but nothing has been confirmed.
