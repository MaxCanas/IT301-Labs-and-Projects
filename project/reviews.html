My Personal Ranking (top 5 albums)

  1. Late Registration (2004)

    This album is his most consistent project in terms of quality. Every song has something to love about it with songs like We Major, Touch the Sky, and Late that are upbeat and funny. There are also songs like Diamonds From Sierra Leone, Roses, and Hey Mama that are talk about more serious topics with a melodic twist to each.

  2. Yeezus (2013)

    I am a huge fan of Kanye's maximalism, and this album is full of unique, industrial sounds that no other artist could replicate or even dream of making. On Sight, Hold My Liqour, and Guilt Trip perfectly capture the chaotic nature of Kanye's creativity.

  3. 808s & Heartbreaks (2008)

    This album has grown on me over time and certainly requires an acquired taste to truly enjoy the project. The production on this album was very ahead of its time, and its easy to see how it inspired much of todays pop music. Love Lockdown, Coldest Winter, and Say You Will are good examples of the slow, singy vibes the album gives off.

  4. Graduation (2007)

    This album has many uplifting, energetic songs with some of the best production exhibited in his entire discography. Songs like Homecoming, Flash Lights, and Champion prove this point, every song is fantastic regardless.

  5. Donda (2021)

    Donda is a very spiritually inspired project with amazing performances from all the artists featured on this album. It also is a good balance of Kanye's religious side and his hip hop-inspired side. Songs like Hurricane, New Again, and Believe What I Say are powerful tracks with satisfy production and verses you wouldn't hear anywhere else.
